JollyOS - READ ME FIRST (Windows)
=================================

Three steps:

  1. Extract this ZIP first. Right-click the downloaded ZIP file, choose
     "Extract All...", and pick a place like your Desktop or Documents.
     JollyOS will NOT run from inside the ZIP.

  2. Open the extracted JollyOS folder and double-click "Start JollyOS".

  3. A black window opens and says JollyOS is running, and your browser opens
     at http://127.0.0.1:3000 (another number if 3000 is busy). Pick a
     username and a password - that is your account.

The warning you will see
------------------------
Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" box, because this download is
new and not signed by a big company. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
Windows only asks the first time.

Good to know
------------
  * Leave the black window open while you use JollyOS. Closing it (or pressing
    Ctrl+C in it) stops JollyOS.
  * Everything you save lives in the "data" folder next to this file. To move
    to a newer version of JollyOS, copy that "data" folder into the new one.
  * JollyOS only listens on your own machine (127.0.0.1). Nobody on your
    network or the internet can reach it.
  * Nothing is installed. The folder contains its own copy of the official
    Node.js runtime. Deleting the folder removes JollyOS completely.
  * If something goes wrong, the reason is in data\server.log.
  * Forgot your password? Open the JollyOS folder, hold Shift and right-click
    in it, choose "Open in Terminal" or "Open PowerShell window here", and run:
        runtime\node.exe --experimental-strip-types app\scripts\reset-owner.mjs
